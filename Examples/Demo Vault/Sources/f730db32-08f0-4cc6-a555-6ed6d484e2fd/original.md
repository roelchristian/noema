¹ I sing the Fire-Eye of the Diphyes, the Two-in-One, the Twin-Hearted, the Gate-Rending Eye, the Eye of the Great Judge; ² the dread Eye that beholdeth the whole world, the Eye of Fire, the lidless stone of destruction. ³ I sing the stone circular Eye, hard and without eyelids, whose rim is as a whetstone; ⁴ the bronze-like Eye that is never dimmed, coal-white and never cooled, the Eye of Fire that is never quenched, keeper of doors and hunter of corners. ⁵ It hangeth where no beam of the roof can reach, yet it looketh down through the hole of smoke and the rift of the bar; ⁶ through the reed-wall and the cleft of the shutter it gazeth, as through a spider’s web. ⁷ It numbereth the living, it numbereth the dying, ⁸ it numbereth the shiver that lieth between. ⁹ In fear I sing, that my little breath may sink and be hidden beneath this Eye that counteth ten thousand times ten thousand.

¹⁰ I cry the Eye of the Diphyes, the iron Eye, the Eye of Fire of the Gate-Rending One, the cold disk of the Great Judge, ¹¹ the wolf’s pupil, the sword’s point, devourer of mysteries. ¹² I cry the eyelidless Eye, the spear-point Eye, the Eye of cold radiance, ¹³ whose slanting glance passeth through stone as through pressed wool. ¹⁴ It seeth the bent nail in the door, the hiding-place beneath the board, the hand that stealeth, the hand that trembleth, ¹⁵ the hand that but dreameth of evil. ¹⁶ It is counter of pulses, graver of glances, ¹⁷ reckoner of hidden coins. ¹⁸ Each little motion of the fearful eyelids it marketh as a cut upon an unseen tablet. ¹⁹ In trembling I cry, that my face may be darkened and unwoven upon the rim of that sharp Eye, which numbereth ten thousand times ten thousand.

²⁰ I wail the Eye of the Diphyes, bright in winter, the Eye of Fire, the Gate-Rending Eye, the balance-stone of the Great Judge; ²¹ the tyrant in the mirror, the beholder of bones, the last witness within. ²² I wail the mirror-Eye, the water-Eye, the Eye of shining metal, and the hidden Eye of Fire within them, ²³ which sitteth in cauldrons and swords and in still water; wheresoever men look, already it looketh. ²⁴ There is no street so narrow for its spear-like sight, there is no night so thick for its fire-like sight, ²⁵ neither mask so exact for its bone-sight. ²⁶ It is pursuer of wildernesses, tracker of multitudes, net-weaver in thought; ²⁷ it hunteth the man that watcheth alone, it hunteth the crowd that is pressed together, ²⁸ it hunteth the man who hideth from his own counsel. ²⁹ In faintness I wail, knowing that there is no corner above it, the many-faced Eye that weigheth myriads of myriads.

³⁰ I groan for the Eye of the Diphyes, the Gate-Rending Eye that bursteth hinges, the Eye of the Great Judge as a stone tablet; ³¹ breaker of doors, smoother of secrets, interpreter of words unspoken. ³² I groan for the Eye that is nail-head and ring and mill-wheel, ³³ beneath whose turning every hidden track lieth level and laid bare. ³⁴ It counteth the unuttered covenant in the twitch of the lip, the swallowed outrage in the hardening of the jaw, ³⁵ the nod given in the dark by the little lift of the shoulder. ³⁶ It is a silent weigher, carver of half-words, engraver upon unseen tablets. ³⁷ Before its gaze doors within or without are rent wide; ³⁸ none abideth half-open. ³⁹ In dread I groan, for all my heart is doors, and all are seen by that untiring Eye of Fire, which marketh myriads of myriads.

⁴⁰ I lament the Eye of the Diphyes, the hammer’s Eye, the Eye of Fire of the Gate-Rending One, the seal-stone Eye of the Great Judge; ⁴¹ the circle of fate, the sleepless memory, the lord of the last beholding. ⁴² I lament the hard, sleepless circular Eye, the unrusted nail-head, the whetstone without dust, ⁴³ keeper of yokes, counter of steps, bearer of the marks of causes. ⁴⁴ Whomsoever it looketh on, they are cloven in twain as doors are broken by a ram; ⁴⁵ all hidden things are poured naked to the earth. ⁴⁶ They whom it passeth by are few, ⁴⁷ and these know not whether they are spared, or only kept in its thought. ⁴⁸ It forgetteth nothing that ever it hath smitten with its glance, ⁴⁹ but setteth all things on an unbending balance. ⁵⁰ In hopelessness I lament, knowing that neither hiding nor escape nor secret sinking down is possible from beneath this one circular all-remembering Eye of Fire, seeing and numbering ten thousand times ten thousand.
